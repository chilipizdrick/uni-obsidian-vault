### Ершов Алексей Геннадьевич КМБО-03-23

### Вариант 5

$$\begin{gather}
\begin{cases}
u_{tt} - \Delta u = 0 \\
u|_{t=0} = x^{2} - y^{2} \\
u_{t}|_{t=0} = xy^{3}
\end{cases}
\end{gather}$$

## Решение

$$\begin{gather}
\Delta \phi = \Delta(x^{2} - y^{2}) = 1 - 1 = 0 \implies \phi(x,y) - \text{гарм.}
\end{gather}$$

$$\begin{gather}
\Delta \psi = \Delta(xy^{3}) = 6xy \neq 0 \implies \psi(x,y) - \text{не гарм.}
\end{gather}$$

Замена:

$$\begin{gather}
u = v + \phi \\
\begin{cases}
v_{tt} - \Delta v = 0 \\
v|_{t=0} = 0 \\
v_{t}|_{t=0} = xy^{3}
\end{cases}
\end{gather}$$

$$\begin{gather}
v(x,y,t) = \frac{1}{2\pi}\iint_{r < t}  \, \frac{\psi(x,y)d\xi d\eta}{\sqrt{ t^{2}-r^{2}}}, \\
r = |\xi - x|^{2} + |\eta - y|^{2}
\end{gather}$$
