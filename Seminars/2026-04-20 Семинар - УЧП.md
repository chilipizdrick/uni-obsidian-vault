$$\begin{gather}
\vec{r} = (x,y,z) \\
\begin{cases}
u_{tt} - a^{2} \Delta u = f(\vec{r}, t), \ \vec{r} \in \mathbb{R}^{3}, t >0 \\
u|_{t=0} = \phi(\vec{r}), \ u_{t}|_{t=0} = \psi(\vec{r})
\end{cases}
\end{gather}$$

## Формула Кирхгофа


$$\begin{gather}
u(\vec{r}, t) = \frac{1}{4\pi a^{2}} \frac{ \partial }{ \partial t } \left( \frac{1}{t} \iint_{R = at} \phi(\tilde{\vec{r}}) dS  \right) + \frac{1}{4\pi a^{2}t}\iint_{R = at} \Psi(\tilde{\vec{r}})dS + \\
+ \frac{1}{4\pi a^{2}}\iiint_{R < at} \frac{1}{R}f\left( \tilde{\vec{r}}, t - \frac{R}{a} \right) dV, \ R = |\vec{r} - \tilde{\vec{r}}|
\end{gather}$$
