$$\begin{gather}
f(x) = \begin{cases}
1, x(t) \geq 0, x_{(t)a}\not\equiv 0, \\
0, x(t) \equiv 0, \\
-1, \exists t: x(t) < 0
\end{cases}
\end{gather}$$

$$\begin{gather}
f(x) = \int_{-1}^{1} x(t) \, dx \\
||x|| \leq 1 \\
\end{gather}$$

$$\begin{gather}
|f(x)| = \left|\int_{-1}^{1} x(t) \, dx \right| \leq \int_{-1}^{1} |x(t)| \, dx  \leq \int_{-1}^{1} ||x||_{C} \, dx  \leq 2||x||_{C} \leq 2
\end{gather}$$

$$\begin{gather}
|f(x') - f(x'')| = |f(x'-x'')| \leq 2 |x'-x''| \\
\end{gather}$$

Т.е норма функционала $\leq 2$
