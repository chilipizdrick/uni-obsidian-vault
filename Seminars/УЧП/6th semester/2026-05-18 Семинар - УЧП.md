## Интегральные уравнения

Уравнение Фредгольма:

$$\begin{gather}
\int_{a}^{b}y(t) K(x,t) \, dt = f(x) \\
Ay = f \\
y = \underbrace{A^{-1}}_{?}f
\end{gather}$$

Уравнение Вольтерра:

$$\begin{gather}
\int_{a}^{x} y(t) K(x,t) \, dt = f(x)
\end{gather}$$


### Частный случай 1

$$\begin{gather}
a = -\infty, b = +\infty, K(x,t) = K(x-t) \\
\underbrace{\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} y(t)K(x-t) \, dt}_{\text{свертка}} = f(x)
\end{gather}$$

$$\begin{gather}
\hat{y}(\omega) = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} y(t)e^{-i\omega t} \, dt - \text{пр. Фурье} \\
y(t) = \frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} \hat{y}(\omega) e^{i\omega x} \, d\omega
\end{gather}$$

Свойство преобразования Фурье:

$$\begin{gather}
\hat{y}(\omega)\hat{K}(\omega) = \hat{f}(\omega)
\end{gather}$$

### Пример 1

$$\begin{gather}
\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} y(t) e^{-(x-t)^{2}} \, dt = e^{-x^{2}/2} \\
\hat{f} (\omega) = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} e^{-x^{2}/2}e^{-i\omega x} \, dx = \\
= \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} e^{-\frac{1}{2} (x^{2} + 2i\omega x + (i\omega)^{2} - (i\omega)^{2})} \, dx = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} e^{-\frac{1}{2} (x + i\omega)^{2}} e^{-\omega^{2}/2} \, dx = \\
\end{gather}$$

$$\begin{gather}
= e^{-\omega^{2}/2} \int_{-\infty + i\omega}^{+\infty + i\omega} e^{-t^{2}/2} \, dt = \sqrt{ 2\pi } \cdot e^{\omega^{2}/2}
\end{gather}$$

$$\begin{gather}
\hat{K}(\omega) = \sqrt{ \pi  e^{-\omega^{2}/4}}
\end{gather}$$

Воспользуемся свойством:

$$\begin{gather}
\hat{y}(\omega) = \frac{\sqrt{ 2 }}{\sqrt{ \pi }} \cdot \sqrt{ \pi } e^{-\omega^{2}/4} \\
y(x) = \sqrt{ \frac{2}{\pi} }e^{-x^{2}}
\end{gather}$$

$$\begin{gather}
\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} y(t) \mathcal{X}_{\left[ -\frac{1}{2}, \frac{1}{2} \right]} (x-t) \, dt = (1-|x|) \mathcal{X}_{[-1, 1]}(x) \\
K(x) = \mathcal{X}_{\left[ -\frac{1}{2}, \frac{1}{2} \right]}(x) \\
\end{gather}$$

$$\begin{gather}
\hat{K}(\omega) = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} \mathcal{X}_{\left[ -\frac{1}{2}, \frac{1}{2} \right]}e^{-i\omega x} \, dx = \int_{- 1/2}^{1/2} e^{-i\omega x} \, dx = -\frac{1}{i\omega} (e^{-i\omega /2} - e^{i\omega /2}) = \\
= \frac{2}{\omega} \sin \frac{\omega}{2}
\end{gather}$$

$$\begin{gather}
\hat{f}(\omega) = \int_{-1}^{1} (1 - |x|) e^{-i\omega x} \, dx = \int_{-1}^{1} (1- |x|)(\cos\omega x - i \sin \omega x) \, dx = \\

$$\begin{gather}
= 2 \int_{0}^{1} (1-x)\cos (\omega x) \, dx  = \dots = \frac{4}{\omega^{2}} \sin ^{2} \frac{\omega}{2}

$$\begin{gather}
\hat{y}(\omega) \cdot \frac{2}{\omega} \sin \left( \frac{\omega}{2} \right) = \frac{4}{\omega^{2}} \sin ^{2}\left( \frac{\omega}{2} \right) \\
