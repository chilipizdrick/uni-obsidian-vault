$$\begin{gather}
\begin{cases}
u_{t} = a^{2}u_{xx} + f(t,x), \\
u|_{t=0} = \phi(x), \ x \in (-\infty,\infty)
\end{cases} \\
\end{gather}$$

> [!definition] Формула Пуассона
> $$\begin{gather}
u = u_{1} + u_{2} \\
u_{1} = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{ \pi t }} \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \phi(y) e^{-\frac{(x-y)^{2}}{4t}}\, dy \\
u_{2} = \int_{0}^{t} \frac{d\tau}{2\sqrt{ \pi(t-\tau) }} \cdot \int dy \cdot f(\tau,y) \cdot e^{-\frac{(x-y)^{2}}{4(t-\tau)}}
\end{gather}$$

### Задача 1

$$\begin{gather}
f(t,x) = te^{-3t} \\
u_{1} = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{ \pi t }}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-4y^{2}-y} \sin(3y)e^{-\frac{(x-y)^{2}}{4t}} \, dy = \\
= \frac{1}{2\sqrt{ \pi t }}\left[\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-4y^{2}-y+3iy-(x-y)^{2}/4t} \, dy - К.С. \right] \\
\end{gather}$$

$$\begin{gather}
I = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-4y^{2}-y+3iy-(x-y)^{2}/4t} \, dy \\
-4y^{2}-y+3iy-\frac{(x-y)^{2}}{4t} = \\
= \frac{-16ty^{2} - 2ty + i\cdot 12ty - x^{2} + 2xy - y^{2}}{4t} = \\
= \frac{-y^{2}(1+16t) - y(4t-i\cdot 12t - 2x)}{4t} - \frac{x^{2}}{4t} = \\
\end{gather}$$

$$\begin{gather}
= -(1+16t)\left( y^{2} + \frac{2y}{1+16t}(2t-i6t-x) \right) - \frac{x^{2}}{4t} = \\
= -\frac{1+16t}{4t}\left( y^{2} + 2y\cdot \frac{2t-i 6t-x}{1+16t} + \left[\frac{2t-i 6t-x}{1+16t}\right]^{2}  \right)
\end{gather}$$
