## Элипрические уравнения (примеры)

### Метод разделения переменных

$$\begin{gather}
u = u(x,y) \\
\Omega = (0,a) \times (0,b) \\
\end{gather}$$

Задача Дирихле:

$$\begin{gather}
u_{xx} + u_{yy} = 0 \\
u(0,y) = 0 \\
u(a,y) = 0 \\
u(x,0) = \phi(x) \\
u(x,b) = \psi(x)
\end{gather}$$

$$\begin{gather}
u(x,y) = X(x)Y(y) \\
u(x,y) = X(x)Y(y) \to u_{xx} + u_{yy} = 0 \\
(X(x)Y(y))_{xx} + (X(x)Y(y))_{yy} = 0 \\
\left.X''Y + XY'' = 0 \right| : XY \\
\frac{X''}{X} + \frac{Y''}{Y} = 0 \\
\frac{X''}{X} = -\frac{Y''}{Y} = \lambda = \mathrm{const}
\end{gather}$$
