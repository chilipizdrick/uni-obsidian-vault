### Кинематические связи:

Т.к. МЦС тела 3 - точка его касания $K$ с наклонной плоскостью:

$$\begin{gather}
v_{1} = v_{2} = v_{3} = 2R\omega_{3} \\
v_{C} = R\omega_{3} \\
2R \cdot \omega_{3} = r \cdot \omega_{2} \implies \omega_{2} = \frac{2R}{r}\omega_{3}
\end{gather}$$

### 1. $T = T_{1} + T_{2} + T_{3}$

$$\begin{gather}
T_{1} = \frac{1}{2}m_{1}v_{1}^{2} = \frac{1}{2}m_{1} v_{2}^{2} = 2m_{1} R^{2}\omega_{3}^{2}
\end{gather}$$

$$\begin{gather}
T_{2} = \frac{1}{2}J_{2}\omega_{2}^{2} = \frac{1}{2}\left( \frac{1}{2}m_{2}r^{2} \right)\omega_{2}^{2} = \\
= \frac{1}{4}m_{2}r^{2}\left( \frac{2R}{r} \omega_{3} \right)^{2} = m_{2}R^{2}\omega_{3}^{2}
\end{gather}$$

Т.к. $r$ - радиус инерции тела $3$ $\implies$ $J_{C} = m_{3}r^{2}$.

$$\begin{gather}
T_{3} = \frac{1}{2}m_{3}v_{C}^{2} + \frac{1}{2}J_{C}\omega_{3}^{2} = \frac{1}{2}m_{3}(R\omega_{3})^{2} + \frac{1}{2}(m_{3}r^{2})\omega_{3}^{2} = \\
= \frac{1}{2}m_{3}(R^{2} + r^{2})\omega_{3}^{2}
\end{gather}$$

$$\begin{gather}
T = T_{1} + T_{2} + T_{3} \\
T = \underbrace{\left( 2m_{1}R^{2} + m_{2}R^{2} + \frac{1}{2}m_{3}(R^{2} + r^{2}) \right)}_{L} \omega_{3}^{2}
\end{gather}$$
